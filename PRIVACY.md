# Privacy policy

LightTabs does not collect, send or sell any data.

- **What it stores:** your settings, your saved tab lists (address and title of each tab), and when each open tab was last used. This is only used to decide which tabs to suspend and to show your lists.
- **Suspended tabs:** a suspended tab's own address contains the page's address and title, so it shows up in your browser history like any other page.
- **Where:** in your browser's extension storage on your device. If you have browser sync turned on, your settings (not your saved lists) sync through your browser account, like any extension's settings.
- **Network:** the extension makes no network requests. Site icons come from the browser's own icon cache.
- **YouTube (optional, off by default):** if you turn on "Remember where YouTube videos were", LightTabs asks for access to youtube.com. It then reads only the playing position of the video on a YouTube tab, just before that tab is suspended or saved, and adds it to the tab's address (for example `&t=300s`). Nothing else on the page is read, and nothing is sent anywhere. Turning the option off gives the access back.
- **Removal:** uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored. Export your lists first if you want to keep them.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
